Game Information
Title: Cuphead
URL: https://www.gog.com/game/cuphead

Instructions:
Needs your own copy of Cuphead for macOS from GOG (1.3.4, with or without The Delicious Last
Course). Copy GOG's macOS installers (the .pkg files, game and DLC) or the installed Cuphead.app
into ports/cuphead/gamedata/, then start Cuphead from Ports.
The first launch builds the game on the handheld: it asks for the texture quality (Medium is the
tested one), downloads Unity's Android player from Unity (about 190 MB, needs Wi-Fi), and takes 1 to
3 hours once. About 10 GB free space during the setup. Keep the handheld plugged in; an interrupted
setup continues on the next launch. The setup log is ports/cuphead/patchlog.txt, the game's
ports/cuphead/log.txt.
Needs a 32-bit (armhf) userland with OpenGL ES 3; firmwares without 32-bit libraries cannot run it
yet.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|D-pad| Move; aim while Lock is held |
|B (bottom)| Jump; confirm in menus |
|Y (left)| Shoot |
|A (right)| EX shot; Super Art with five cards; back in menus |
|X (top)| Dash |
|L1| Switch weapon |
|R1| Lock (stand still and aim) |
|Start| Pause |
|Select + Start| Quit |
