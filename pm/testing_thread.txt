Game Information
Title: Cuphead
URL: https://www.gog.com/game/cuphead

Instructions:
Needs your own copy of Cuphead for macOS from GOG (1.3.4, with or without The Delicious Last
Course). Copy GOG's macOS installers (the .pkg files, game and DLC) or the installed Cuphead.app
into ports/cuphead/gamedata/, then start Cuphead from Ports.
The first launch builds the game on the handheld: it asks for the texture quality (Low, the
recommended one, makes it shorter), needs no Wi-Fi (Unity's Android player comes with the port), and takes 1 to
3 hours once. About 12 GB free space during the setup. Keep the handheld plugged in; an interrupted
setup continues on the next launch. The setup log is ports/cuphead/patchlog.txt, the game's
ports/cuphead/log.txt.
Needs a 64-bit (aarch64) system with OpenGL ES 3. The port is 64-bit only: no 32-bit libraries
needed. Over an earlier release's 32-bit game: put the game in gamedata again and launch; the 64-bit
setup runs once and the saves are copied over.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|D-pad| Move; aim while Lock is held |
|B (bottom)| Jump; confirm in menus |
|Y (left)| Shoot |
|A (right)| EX shot; Super Art with five cards; back in menus |
|X (top)| Dash |
|L1| Switch weapon |
|R1| Lock (stand still and aim) |
|Start| Pause |
|Select + Start| Quit |
