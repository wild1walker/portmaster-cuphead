Put your copy of Cuphead here, then launch Cuphead from the Ports menu:

  - the installed game: Cuphead.app (GOG, macOS version 1.3.4; with The Delicious Last Course
    if you have it), copied from a Mac,
  - or GOG's macOS installers (.pkg files: the game, and the DLC's).

Optional: UnitySetup-Android-Support-for-Editor-2017.4.9f1.exe (from Unity's download archive);
otherwise the setup downloads it from Unity (about 190 MB, Wi-Fi needed).
The first launch builds the game on the handheld: one to a few hours, once. See ../README.md.
